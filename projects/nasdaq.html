CASE STUDY / MACHINE LEARNING

NASDAQ-100 forecasting

Combining stock prices and macroeconomic indicators to explore next-day market direction and returns.

PythonXGBoostTime series
View source code ↗Read the report ↗

01 / THE QUESTION

Start with the problem.

Can historical stock prices and macroeconomic signals help explain next-day NASDAQ-100 movements?

02 / THE PROCESS

From raw data to a useful solution.

Combined data for 20 constituent stocks with macroeconomic indicators, engineered lagged and rolling features, and trained separate XGBoost models for direction and returns. The evaluation used a chronological training and test split.

03 / THE OUTCOME

What the work showed.

The published classification experiment reported 74% accuracy and a bullish-class F1 score of 0.78. Lagged returns and rolling market features contributed more than macroeconomic variables to short-term predictions.

04 / THE PERSPECTIVE

Interpret the result in context.

These are historical test results, not evidence of future trading performance. Macroeconomic release timing, look-ahead bias and changing market conditions require careful validation.
